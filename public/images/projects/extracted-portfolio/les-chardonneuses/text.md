COLLABRATION HEADPIECE DESIGN | GERMANIER PARIS HAUTE COUTURE SS26
12/2025- 01/2026 / SITE: Paris, France
A couture headpiece created for Germanier Paris Haute Couture SS26, developed as a wearable micro-structure inspired by the
flower’s lifecycle—growth, collapse, and decay. At Ivy J Studio, I was responsible for translating the initial artistic direction into a
structurally stable, lightweight construction, resolving how the piece could hold its form on the body without failure.
As the assistant designer, I supported design decisions through fast 3D iteration and parametric exploration, developing lace-like
digtial textures and controlled deformations that simulate organic rupture. Through repeated 3D printing tests and prototyping, we
refined the internal framework and surface geometry, aligning digital complexity with real material behaviour to deliver a runway-
ready piece where ornament emerges from structure.
