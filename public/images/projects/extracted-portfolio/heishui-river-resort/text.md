Heishui River Resort
Heishui River Resort is a hospitality project that merges nature, architecture, and human experience. Inspired by the Blackwater River, the design creates seasonal, water-adjacent spaces that integrate local culture,
wellness, and immersive tourism. With around 100 hotel rooms and 50 boutique homestays, the architecture blends dark tones, mountain-inspired forms, and native vegetation to harmonize with the river landscape.
As part of the design team, I was involved in the early concept stage, contributing to site planning, room layout strategies, and the development of spatial concepts that guided the overall
masterplan. Our focus was on creating a resort that retreats into its environment while enhancing guest experience through circulation, micro-ecosystems, and a symbiotic relationship with
the surrounding ecology. The project has since won the competition and is currently under construction in China, positioning itself as both a cultural destination and an ecological landscape.

During the early design stage of the Heishui River Resort, I was responsible for developing the overall spatial
planning and refining the layout of different room typologies and public buildings. Working closely with the
design director, I proposed the initial massing strategy that responded to the site’s natural topography and river
orientation, ensuring a seamless transition between private villas, shared courtyards, and communal facilities. My
role involved translating conceptual massing into detailed spatial plans—optimising circulation, view corridors, and
spatial hierarchy to balance privacy and openness across the resort. The resulting layout established a coherent
relationship between architecture and landscape, forming the foundation for the project’s later development and
successful competition outcome.
